# Credits and sources

Everything in Aircraft Explorer that was not created for this project, and where it came from.

## 1. Photographs

All 77 photographs are from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/). For each one, the author, license, and any usage restrictions were checked through the Commons API before download. Only freely licensed files were used (CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC0, or public domain), and files flagged for identifiable people were excluded. Copies are stored locally in `public/photos/`; the site makes no requests to Wikimedia.

**Changes made:** every photo was resized. Most are also cropped by the page layouts to fit their frames. Photos under CC BY-SA licenses remain under their original licenses.

**Licenses used:** 25 × CC BY-SA 4.0, 18 × CC BY 4.0, 12 × CC BY-SA 2.0, 6 × CC0, 6 × CC BY-SA 3.0, 4 × CC BY 2.0, 3 × CC BY-SA 3.0 au, 1 × Public domain, 1 × CC BY-SA 3.0 de, 1 × CC BY 2.5.

## 4. Aircraft information

The aircraft content was written for this project with the help of an AI coding agent. It covers specifications, history, design notes, operators, "how to recognize it" notes, and facts. It is based on general knowledge of manufacturer-published specifications and widely reported aviation history. **It was not checked against specific live sources.** Figures are rounded and labelled as approximate where they depend on configuration.

For verification, the primary references are the manufacturers' own aircraft pages ([Airbus](https://aircraft.airbus.com/) and [Boeing](https://www.boeing.com/commercial)). They are listed here as suggested references; they were not consulted while writing the content.

Seat diagrams are illustrative: they are generated from typical layouts and are not airline seat maps.
